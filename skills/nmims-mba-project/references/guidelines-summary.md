# NMIMS CDOE project rules (Semester IV, 6 credits)

Source: `NMIMS_Project_Guidelines_Sem3_Sem4.pdf` in this folder ("Project Prework Student Communications, Sem 3 and Sem 4"). Re-read the PDF if a new cycle's version is issued; rules can change.

## Must-haves (failing these means zero or "not graded")
- **Format must match** the one selected at registration (Research-Based Project, Business Plan, or Problem-Solving Proposal). Mismatch with the selected format or specialisation: the project is not graded.
- **Title** at most 20 words, reflects the format, aligned with the specialisation (Business Management may cover any area; Finance, HR, Marketing, Operations & Data Science must stay within their field). The cover title must match the title registered on the portal.
- **Originality.** Scanned against other students, the web and Gen-AI. Above the university's tolerance: graded zero. Cite every secondary source under References.
- **PDF only**, typed (no handwritten or scanned pages). One main document; supporting material (charts, survey data, screenshots, transcripts, spreadsheets, PowerPoint, links) goes inside it as **annexures**.
- **Submit online only** via Student Zone, Exam, Project Submission, after separate project registration and fee payment (Rs 800). No late submissions. Pass mark 40/100.

## Suggested layout (follow exactly)
| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Length | 30-60 pages overall; 10-20 pages per section (guidance) |
| Body font | Calibri or Arial, 11 pt |
| Section headings | 14 pt bold |
| Sub-headings | 12 pt bold |
| Table text | 10 pt |
| Margins | 1 inch all sides |
| Line spacing | 1.15 |
| Paragraph spacing | 6 pt before and after |
| Alignment | Paragraphs justified; titles centred |
| Page numbers | Bottom centre, "Page X of Y" |

The build in `template/tools/build_pdf.mjs` applies all of these. The cover is unnumbered; numbering starts on the contents page.

## Semester III pre-work (optional, recommended)
Choose specialisation and format, submit the title and description on the portal (Exams > Project Pre-work), attend faculty sessions from week 4. Mentoring is not given at the last minute; "Post My Query" closes 15 days before the deadline.
