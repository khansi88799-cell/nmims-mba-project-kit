# Questions to ask the student

Ask in one batch, early. Tell the student that short, rough answers in their own words are best, and that anything that did not happen should be answered "no". Write the reflection and the personal parts of the method only from these answers.

## Admin (needed for the cover and to avoid "not graded")
1. Exact title registered on the student portal.
2. Format selected (Research / Business Plan / Problem-Solving) and specialisation.
3. Full name, SAP ID, exam cycle (e.g. December 2026), faculty guide (or none).
4. Is the organisation real and can it be named? What data or documents can be used?
5. Can a short survey or interviews be done before the deadline? (If not, design the report so it does not depend on them.)

## Why this topic
6. Personal connection: a real incident, roughly what happened, amount, how it ended.
7. Day job and how it shaped the work.

## Decisions
8. An idea you started with and changed. Why?
9. The hardest trade-off and what tipped it.
10. Any design choice you insisted on, and your reasoning.

## Working with others (Problem-Solving rubric 9)
11. Who helped or gave input (friends, colleagues, guide, family, users)? What did each contribute?
12. Disagreement or critical feedback received, your response, and whether it changed anything.
13. Anything you led (discussions, deadlines, presenting). If nothing yet, what you plan to lead next.

## Learning
14. What surprised you most?
15. What would you do differently?
16. How will this help your career?
